How to use this course

Monday, Wednesday and Friday introduce the three core lessons each week. Tuesday and Thursday reinforce the preceding lesson. Saturday is a practical recording and review session. Sunday is an optional free jam or rest day.

Practice 30–45 minutes: 5 minutes warm-up, 10 minutes theory, 10–15 minutes focused playing and 5–15 minutes jamming. Slow down whenever notes sound unclear. A metronome is helpful.

Completion and notes are saved using this browser's local storage, not an account. Keep a backup using Export. iOS may handle downloaded HTML differently depending on where it is opened.
